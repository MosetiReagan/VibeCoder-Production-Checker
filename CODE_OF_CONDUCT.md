# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community for everyone.

## Standards

- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Respect different viewpoints and experience
- Accept constructive criticism
- Focus on what is best for the community
- Show empathy toward other participants

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, trolling, discriminatory jokes or images, publishing others' private information, and unwelcome sexual attention are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report misconduct to conduct@vibecoder.dev. Maintainers may remove comments, issues, or contributions and ban repeat offenders.

This code adapts the widely used Contributor Covenant approach.
